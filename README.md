## 🐍 Latest Problem

### Q26 — Sum of All Even Numbers from 1 to n

**Problem Statement:**

Write a Python program to calculate the sum of all even numbers from 1 to a user-provided number `n`.

**Example:**

```text
Input: 10
Output: 30
```

Explanation: The even numbers from 1 to 10 are 2, 4, 6, 8, and 10.

Their sum is 30.

**Concepts Practiced:**
